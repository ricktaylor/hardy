## Dispatch/delivery state-machine rationalisation (`fix/bpa-dispatch-redelivery`, in flight)

Symmetric local and remote egress on the current status-based lifecycle, using the forwarding path (`ForwardPending → ForwardAckPending`) as the template for delivery. Triggered by the dispatch-queue duplicate re-delivery bug (the storage channel's poller re-injected in-flight `Dispatching` bundles → unbounded concurrent duplicate `on_deliver`s); root-causing it exposed two further latent defects that get fixed identically in both paths.

**Decisions of record (Rick, 2026-08-26):** delivery aligns with forwarding, no divergence — new `DeliverPending { service }` (per-service queue, mirror of the per-peer `ForwardPending` channel) and `DeliveryAckPending { service }` (in service's hands, no poller, every exit resolves the claim); `WaitingForService` keeps its distinct meaning "there is currently no service for this bundle" (analogue of `Waiting`); a bundle discovered to be local parks `WaitingForService`, never `Waiting` (today a local destination with no registered service falls into the generic no-route park); the per-service queue lands now, not as a follow-up; latent forwarding bugs are fixed identically in both places. **Truthful delivery resolution:** the reaper must not race a commenced delivery — a bundle expiring mid-`on_deliver` that the service consumes must report *delivered*, not "deleted: lifetime expired" (the BPA must not lie). So the reaper defers `DeliveryAckPending` (the delivery resolves its own outcome; a deferral re-parks and then expires honestly; the consumer never *commences* delivery of an already-expired bundle). **Revised same day (Rick):** `ForwardAckPending` defers too, for symmetry — the reaper excludes both hand-off states (work in an external party's hands cannot be recalled, so blanket reaping makes the BPA lie), and expiry is enforced at checkpoints instead: dispatch gains an expiry check at the top of `process_bundle` (covering the `Failed`-outcome re-entry, sweeps, and polls), delivery keeps its pre-`on_deliver` check. The `poll_expiry` contract stays simple — no status filtering pushed into the storage impls; the reaper's refill skips the deferred statuses so a held-open hand-off cannot spin the refill loop (their exits re-arm the watch via `park_bundle`/`watch_bundle`). The wider "reap only `Waiting`/`WaitingForService`" alternative was considered and rejected: `AduFragment` and backpressured `*Pending` queues are also unbounded pauses, and reaping queued states is always truthful under the claim-CAS discipline.

**The rationalised machine:** `New → DispatchPending → Dispatching` (transient routing decision), then remote: `ForwardPending{peer,queue} → ForwardAckPending{peer}` / local: `DeliverPending{svc} → DeliveryAckPending{svc}`; no opportunity parks remote → `Waiting`, local → `WaitingForService{svc}`; success = conditional `tombstone_if`, failure = conditional park; endpoint loss sweeps queued bundles back to the park state (`reset_peer_queue`/`reset_peer_ack_pending` ↔ new `reset_service_queue` on unregister); (re)registration recovers via channel-creation initial poll (`*Pending`) + `poll_waiting`/`poll_service_waiting` (parked). Invariant: channel-target statuses never outlive their channel — restart maps `ForwardPending`/`ForwardAckPending → Waiting` and `DeliverPending`/`DeliveryAckPending → WaitingForService`; `DispatchPending` re-enqueues. Per-service delivery serializes (in-order, one at a time), matching per-peer egress; delivery stops consuming dispatch processing-pool slots. Remaining deliberate asymmetry: no `transfer_outcome` analogue for services (`on_deliver` is synchronous).

**Latent bugs fixed in both paths:** (1) park-vs-poll race — no park site re-checks after parking, so an opportunity event landing while the bundle is in-flight (`*AckPending`, invisible to that event's poll snapshot) is missed when the failure parks afterwards; fix = capture the `Arc<RouteTable>` snapshot when the flight begins (every RIB mutation publishes a fresh Arc; identity is the change discriminator, held Arc = no ABA), and after *winning* a park, re-dispatch once iff the table changed (`Dispatcher::park_bundle` helper; loop-free — preserves d4bde1f8's no-spin rationale, and retires the ×20 re-register loop in `on_deliver.rs` and the 100 ms quiesce in `pipeline.rs`). (2) d4bde1f8 regression — `forward_bundle`'s `NoNeighbour`/sync-`Err` arms park with **unconditional** `update_status(→ Waiting)` from a stale `ForwardAckPending` snapshot (can stomp a live re-dispatched copy or resurrect a tombstone); restored to conditional `swap_status`, same for the `admin.rs` `WaitingForService` park. (3) stranding delivery exits — Deliver-filter `Err` and BPSec `NoKey` leave the bundle unrecoverable; both now park `WaitingForService` under the claim-resolution contract.

## Remaining streaming work (descendant branch)

**`BundleStorage::save_stream` is the only remaining prerequisite for true streaming.** When it lands, revisit `max_bundle_size`: the 64 MiB default guards in-memory accumulation and should lift to a much larger (or unlimited-with-opt-in) default once bytes spool — the knob's meaning shifts to custody-admission policy (a half-arrived transfer has no metadata entry, so the reaper and eviction cannot touch it; the spool chokepoint still needs the bound). True retirement waits on chunk-capable BPSec (verify/decrypt currently need the whole bundle resident) plus dynamic storage-headroom admission. The ingress drain still accumulates the payload tail in memory before `save`; `BundleStorage` exposes only `save(Bytes)` / `load -> Option<Bytes>`, so there is nowhere to spool a payload tail without materialising it. Add `save_stream` (a push-side `StreamIn<Bytes>` mirroring `recover`'s `Sender` pattern) across `bundle_mem`, `localdisk`, and `sqlite`, plus the `store.rs` wrapper, then swap the in-memory accumulator in `drain_payload` for it.

**Async payload verifier** (deferred until load-side streaming exists): a corrupted payload is not detected until delivery and occupies storage until then; a background task streaming the stored payload could fail it early or stamp "payload verified" in the metadata.

## Queue tranche — the mechanism layer (`queue_architecture.md`)

The queue architecture is a load-bearing streaming component: it replaces the status-based lifecycle with durable queue assignment, and its pull-based `dequeue` deletes the hybrid channel's poller scaffolding (per-cycle channel, spawned forwarding task, cancel-token race) that the streaming pipeline currently threads through. The policy tranche's FlowControllers are the discipline over this mechanism — this tranche must land (or at least its trait split) before the seats bind. Sequencing: after the v0.3.0 stack (the metadata partition's interim `status` field is what this tranche deletes), interleaving with filter Phase 2 (independent surfaces).
